Delivery Planner · Owner's Guide

How To Use It (Full Version)

This is your complete reference. It covers building plans, using it underway, recording trips, and managing the master stop list — including the GitHub steps only your token-equipped devices can do.

One-time setup on each device

1Open the planner at your GitHub Pages URL.
2Scroll to Master List → GitHub connection settings. Enter the repo (genius2go/delivery-planner), branch main, path stops.json, and your personal access token. Tap Save token to this device.
Important: make sure the repo field is filled before saving — a blank repo is the one thing that silently breaks saving. The token lives only on that device; devices without it are read-only.

Building a plan

1Client & Boat: enter vessel, client/owner, the boat's max range per leg, and cruise speed. Pick direction (north or southbound).
2Pick Stops: the list is in geographic order. Tap the stops this client wants — they auto-sequence along the corridor.
3Sequence: drag to reorder, or remove stops, as conditions dictate. This is where you adapt when weather changes.
4Generate Plan: get leg-by-leg distances, hours, range warnings (red if a leg exceeds the boat's range), per-leg maps, and the Notice to Mariners districts for the route. Print/Save PDF for the client; Export saves a JSON copy.

Adding a stop to the master list (your devices only)

1In Master List → Add, edit, or remove a stop, leave the dropdown on "+ Add a new stop."
2Fill in name, state, lat/lon (or tap Use current location), type, all-weather, and notes.
3Tap Save to master → GitHub. It commits to stops.json and appears everywhere on next load.
To edit or delete a stop, pick it from the same dropdown, change the fields (or tap Delete), and save. This is how you build out notes over time — no editing raw files by hand.

Underway — duck into an unplanned port

In the Sequence area, the Underway box lets you add a port that isn't on your list: name, rough position (or fix it later), quick note. It drops into the sequence in geographic order.

Recording the trip as it happens

1Each stop in your Sequence has a ✓ Confirm button. When you pull in for the night, tap it, set the date (auto-fills today), add a quick note on how it went, and save. The stop turns green.
2Every confirm saves to active_trip.json automatically — close the browser, switch devices, go offline; it resumes where you left off.
3At the end, open Delivery Report, fill in dates/crew/summary. The stop log builds itself from your confirmed stops. Toggle Include per-stop notes off for a clean client report, on for your full record.
4Save to trip log archives it to trips.json and offers to clear the active trip for the next delivery.
The summary always shows in the report (your client-facing overview). The per-stop notes are hidden unless you check the box — but they're always kept in your trip log regardless.

Files in your repo

  • index.html — the app itself
  • stops.json — your master stop list (the knowledge base)
  • trips.json — archive of completed deliveries (auto-created)
  • active_trip.json — the in-progress trip (auto-created, cleared when you finish)
Always navigate on current official charts, ATON, and live weather. This tool supplements planning — it never replaces them.
